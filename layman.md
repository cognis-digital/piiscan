piiscan is a command-line tool that scans your data files (such as CSV spreadsheets) and flags columns that likely contain personal information like email addresses, social security numbers, phone numbers, or credit card numbers. It tells you which columns are problematic, how confident it is, and how serious the risk is, so you know exactly where sensitive data is hiding. You can use it manually before sharing a file, plug it into automated pipelines to catch PII before it reaches the wrong place, or let AI agents call it to check data on your behalf. It is aimed at data engineers, analysts, and developers who need a fast, no-fuss way to audit datasets for privacy and compliance purposes.
